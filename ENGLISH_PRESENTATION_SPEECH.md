## ❓ 4. Stage Q&A Preparation (English)

### **Question 1: Does this respect Church canons and traditions?**
> **Answer:** *"Absolutely. The platform does not alter any spiritual or liturgical rites. It solely digitizes financial stewardship (Asrat, Bekuart) and administrative communication."*

### **Question 2: How do non-smartphone users or elderly parishioners contribute?**
> **Answer:** *"Elderly or non-smartphone users can pay in cash at the parish office as usual. The treasurer simply logs it into the Web Portal, which generates a printed physical receipt for them."*

### **Question 3: How does it support overseas Diaspora members?**
> **Answer:** *"Through integrated payment gateways like Chapa and international card processing, members living abroad can contribute directly in foreign currency, which settles into the parish bank account."*
