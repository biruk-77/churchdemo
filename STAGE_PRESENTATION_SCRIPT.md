# 🎤 EOTC Digital Platform — Stage Presentation Script
> **Ultra-Simple 4-Step Script for Stage & Live Demo**

---

## ⚡ Step 1: The Problem & What We Built (1 Minute)

### 🎙️ Spoken Script:
> "Good day, Your Holiness, Respected Fathers, and Guests.
> 
> Currently, traditional church contributions like **Asrat (ዓሥራት - 10% Tithe)**, **Bekuart (በኵራት - Firstfruits)**, and **Selet (ስእለት - Vows)** are collected manually on paper. Diaspora members living abroad also struggle to send their tithes directly to their home parish in Ethiopia.
> 
> To solve this, we built the **EOTC Digital Ecosystem**:
> 1. A **Mobile App** for church members and Diaspora donors.
> 2. A **Web Governance Portal** for Bishops, Diocese Leaders, and Parish Treasurers."

---

## 📱 Step 2: Live Mobile App Demo (2 Minutes)

### 🎙️ Spoken Script:
> "Let's look at the mobile app."

### 🎯 Key Demo Actions to Show on Screen:
1. **2-Step Church Selection:**
   * *Say:* "First, a member selects their **Diocese (ሀገረ ስብከት)** like Hawassa or Addis Ababa, and then selects their parish church, such as **Hawassa St. Gabriel Cathedral**."
2. **Tithes & Payment Integration:**
   * *Say:* "The member enters their monthly income, taps **Calculate Asrat (10%)**, and pays instantly using **Telebirr, CBE Birr, or Chapa**."
3. **Printable Digital Receipt:**
   * *Say:* "Once paid, the app automatically generates an official **printable PDF receipt** with a unique transaction reference number."

---

## 🌐 Step 3: Live Web Admin Portal Demo (2 Minutes)

### 🎙️ Spoken Script:
> "Now let's switch to the Web Governance Portal at `http://localhost:5173`. We have full **Role-Based Access Control**."

### 🎯 Key Demo Actions to Show on Browser:
1. **Click `👑 National` (Holy Synod View):**
   * *Say:* "When logged in as National Synod Admin, we see global contribution analytics across all dioceses in Ethiopia and the Diaspora."
2. **Click `🏛️ Diocese` (Hawassa Archdiocese View):**
   * *Say:* "When logged in as Hawassa Diocese Admin, the portal automatically filters to show ONLY Hawassa's churches, audits, and regional revenue."
3. **Click `⛪ Parish` (Parish Treasurer View):**
   * *Say:* "Parish Treasurers use this view to manage local member rosters and approve offline cash tithes."
4. **Live Push Notification Demo (`/broadcast`):**
   * *Say:* "When an admin sends a broadcast message from the portal, it immediately pops up as a **Live Push Notification Banner** on all members' mobile phones!"

---

## 🏁 Step 4: Closing Summary (30 Seconds)

### 🎙️ Spoken Script:
> "In summary, the EOTC Digital Platform delivers:
> * **100% Financial Transparency** for church leaders.
> * **Seamless Diaspora Contributions** via modern mobile payments.
> * **Role-Based Governance** respecting canonical hierarchy.
> 
> Thank you!"
