## ❓ Anticipated Questions & Answers (Q&A Prep)

### **Q1: Is this canonical and approved for traditional church practices?**
> **Answer:** Yes! The app does not alter any spiritual or canonical rites. It simply digitizes the financial stewardship (Asrat, Bekuart, Selet) and communication aspect, making it easier for members to fulfill their traditional obligations.

### **Q2: What happens if a member is not tech-savvy or doesn't have a smartphone?**
> **Answer:** Parish treasurers can use the Web Portal to manually record cash or offline contributions on behalf of elderly or non-smartphone users, so everyone receives an official digital receipt.

### **Q3: How do we handle security and prevent fraud?**
> **Answer:** The system uses secure phone OTP login, Firebase encrypted cloud databases, and strict access control rules (`firestore.rules`). Members can only view their own transactions, and treasurers can only see data for their assigned parish.

### **Q4: How do members in the Diaspora pay?**
> **Answer:** The app supports multi-channel payment integration via Chapa and digital gateways that accept international cards and wallet transfers, settling directly into the parish's bank account.
